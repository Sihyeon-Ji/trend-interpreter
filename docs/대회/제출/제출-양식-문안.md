## 간략한 설명 (Firebase·Firestore·Cloud Run·Gemini 활용)

```text
Product. Brands already speak in memes, but a stale or misread one is public and hard to take back. Trend Interpreter turns one product into local copy for Korea, Japan and English-language markets. Rules pick a meme that is in use in each market now and safe for a brand, Gemini writes the copy, rules check every line again, and a person approves before anything can be copied. Every meme links to a dictionary entry with its meaning, origin, risk level and sources.

Gemini (Vertex AI). At request time Gemini turns a product photo or one line into a product brief, then writes 4 lines, a video script and a thumbnail plan per market in one structured call, and judges whether copy uses a meme as meant. In the batch pipeline it reads trending Shorts and runs Search-grounded research on new terms. Gemma 4 drafts dictionary translations and classifies evidence sentences for risk. Models never set a status: listing, lifecycle, risk level and ranking are rule code.

Cloud Run. Hosts the Next.js web app, the private FastAPI API, the publication, backup and maintenance jobs and the alert relay. Data lives in Cloud SQL for PostgreSQL; reviewed dictionary changes are published in approved batches.

Firebase / Firestore. Not used – the dictionary is relational and versioned (meanings, evidence, reviews), so it lives in Cloud SQL for PostgreSQL.

Results. On the same 12 product cases across rounds 4 to 9 (10-10 data), picks that fit the product rose from 40% to 91%, no risky meme was shown as safe, and copy usability reached 2.50 out of 3 (AI-drafted judgments reviewed by one team member).
```

## 심사위원 안내 (양식에 메모·추가 정보 칸이 있으면)

심사용 아이디·비밀번호는 저장소에 적지 않는다. 채운 문장은 `artifacts/submission/`에 둔다(커밋하지 않음). 운영 GPU VM은 만들지 않았으므로(10-16 결정) 관리 화면의 Gemma 실시간 버튼은 'GPU 꺼짐'만 알리고 기록된 실행도 없다. 심사위원이 이 버튼을 고장으로 보지 않도록 한 문장 적는다.

```text
Reviewer view (read-only): sign in at /login with user [user] and password [password]. Every admin screen opens – review queue, evidence, change batches, collection status and model records – and approve, reject and risk decisions are refused. The two Gemma buttons (on a dictionary entry and on the status page) answer that the GPU is off: the L4 VM is defined in Terraform but not deployed, to keep the cost down. Gemma's batch work appears in the model records on the status page.
```
